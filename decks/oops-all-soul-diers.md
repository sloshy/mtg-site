description: "Intro\nA twist on the white weenie archetype, this deck aims for protection and life-gain to help preserve your board as long as possible, so you can alpha-strike your opponents with your large number of soldier tokens. Myrel, Shield of Argive  will protect your board state during your turn as commander and prevent your spells from being countered, enabling all kinds of dirty tactics. Meanwhile, as they attack, you double your soldier count which can trigger life-gain effects on other cards and scale up fast enough to become very quickly overwhelming for opponents.\n\nWin-Cons\nThis deck primarily tries to win through regular combat damage by going extremely, exponentially wide. As a backup, the deck has the means to give Myrel the boost they need to deal enough commander damage to opponents, but this is highly unlikely. Instead it is best to try and protect your board by any means necessary and preserve your ability to generate more soldiers.\n\nGeneral Gameplan\nMyrel wants to make as many attacks as possible, so you're going to want to keep them protected and sent over to the least-defensive player at all times. As elaborated on below, Myrel can turn your board into a game-ending threat so long as you have enough creatures in play, which will happen so long as you can make 3-4 swings, ideally with a doubler in play. To help this happen, you will want as much protection as possible to keep them on the board. Standard options like Teferi's Protection or The One Ring will help you live to see another day, but cards like Bastion Protector, Dawn Charm, Galadriel's Dismissal, or Mithril Coat will help you swing in without too much problem. Maze of Ith is a useful utility land that, while typically known for preventing some damage coming your way, can also be used as a means to swing out Myrel without fear of death. Reconnaissance also works for this as well, but without the protective ability of Maze of Ith.\n\nTaking Advantage of Myrel's Protection\nWhen Myrel is out, your opponents can't do anything on your turn that isn't on a land or in their graveyard. This means you are generally safe from instants or boardwipes, so it is a great time to make plays that would otherwise see reprisal. Are you in the lead with the means to win quickly? That's a great time to throw out a Winds of Abandon to wipe the rest of the board and give you the advantage. It's also a great time to cast Teferi's Protection, because it means that you will be completely protected from nearly anything until the end of your next turn!\n\nToken Doubling Payoff\nMyrel will double your soldiers as colorless artifact creature soldier 1/1 tokens. There are plenty of cards out there that double token creation included in this deck such as Mondrak, Glory Dominus and Anointed Procession, or cards that double triggered abilities such as Roaming Throne and Strionic Resonator. You'll want at least one of these on the board at all times to accelerate Myrel's game plan, but it might be worth holding off to seem less threatening at first, only deploying it when you know it will combo with another piece to kill a player.\n\nIn particular, you'll want cards that serve as payoff for the large number of tokens you are creating. One such card that enables your win-con is Goldnight Commander which, even with a modest 6-8 soldiers on the board, is plenty to deal nearly lethal damage to another opponent assuming they swing out. Coat of Arms and Cathars' Crusade  will also greatly buff your entire board, while also being a bit of a PITA to keep track of without organizational skills. For a simpler way to end the game through token payoff, feel free to sink some excess mana into Mirror Entity to nuke another player from orbit while protecting your tokens as they attack with that excess toughness.\n\nEven without sheer buffs, you can sometimes overwhelm your opponents by numbers, swinging out with your extra soldiers given you have  the means to do so. Or, you can play cards like Eldrazi Monument that turn your tokens into scary flyers, or True Conviction that turns your 1/1s into a quick way to gain life and threaten opponents' life totals.\n\nLifegain Subtheme\nThere is a prominent lifegain subtheme to the deck that benefits from large numbers of creatures. Soul Warden, Soul's Attendant and Guide of Souls  will gain you life as creatures enter, while Ajani, Strength of the Pride will generate Ajani's Pridemate tokens and gain you increasingly large life counts. Lastly, Caduceus, Staff of Hermes will turn one of your creatures (ideally Myrel) into an indestructible lifegain powerhouse."
