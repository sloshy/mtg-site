description: >-
  Intro

  This is one of my two Ghyrson Starn, Kelermorph decks. The general idea of
  this commander is that it turns instances of 1 damage into 3 damage, and this
  deck's version of that is as a "spellslinger" style deck that focuses on
  cantrips and payoff for those cantrips. You can see the other version of this
  deck I've built over here.


  Win-Cons

  The primary win-con of this deck is burning the entire table as fast as
  possible while setting up for big turns that do large amount of damage "out of
  nowhere". This means the deck is kind of a glass-cannon, and you will want to
  have certain cards to ensure a quick victory.


  Primarily you're going to want at least some form of lifelink, so that's
  Shadowspear or Basilisk Collar which you can equip on Ghyrson to turn your
  table-wide pings into an extra 2 life per-opponent, per-ping. With this, it's
  pretty easy to set up pingers in such a way that you're draining the table for
  3, 6, even 12 per turn  and gaining at least that much per-ping, quickly
  outpacing them.


  How-To: Stacking Pings

  You're going to want as many pingers on the board at all times, and those
  pingers in this deck for the most part are payoff for casting various
  non-creature spells. For example, having Erebor Flamesmith and Kessig
  Flamebreather out with your commander will turn every 1 or 2-mana cantrip into
  6 damage to the table.


  Spell recursion is important if you want to suddenly "pop off". Once you have
  a decent board state with spellcasting payoff, you'll want to cast as many
  spells as possible, including spells from your graveyard or exile. The most
  impactful way of doing this would be with Mizzix's Mastery overloaded.


  There are some creatures that benefit spell recursion as well. If you ever
  stumble upon Urabrask // The Great Work this is another card you should
  consider always having on the board. It rewards you for casting lots and lots
  of spells by ramping you up as well as pinging, and after casting only three
  spells it flips into board-wide damage, ramp, and will eventually let you cast
  spells from any graveyard. Exalted Flamer of Tzeentch  benefits pinging as
  well as spell-slinging by giving you a random instant/sorcery from your
  graveyard at upkeep to keep the value train going.


  Other Fun Cards & Interactions


  Chandra's Incinerator & Repercussion Combo

  One fun combo that comes up sometimes is Chandra's Incinerator with
  Repercussion. Either of these cards on their own are amazing value by letting
  your translate creature/player damage into the other, and it's important to
  know how the triggers work. For example, with Incinerator, if you deal 1
  damage to an opponent, you get two triggers: 1 damage from Chandra's
  Incinerator to a creature, and 2 damage from Ghyrson. You want to stack these
  so that the Incinerator's trigger resolves firstm and the same goes for
  Repercussion's trigger targeting the opponent. With just one of these
  enchantments out, that's 1 damage to the primary target + 2 from Ghyrson, and
  the secondary target takes a total of 5 (1 damage, plus 2 from Ghyrson, plus 2
  from Ghyrson hitting the primary target). So with both of these enchantments
  out, the ideal way for them to resolve is by stacking the 1-damage triggers on
  top of each other as much as possible.


  The net result is, by prioritizing the 1-damage pings, you will get the
  maximum number of Ghyrson triggers for at least 3x your opponents' toughness
  on board in non-combat damage. So for example, if you hit an opponent for 1
  damage, instead of shortcutting that to 3 damage with Ghyrson out, you would
  have Incinerator deal 1 damage to a creature, then back to the opponent, then
  back to a creature, until all of their creatures are dead. Then you would take
  that many 1-damage pings (1 + total toughness of their board) and multiply
  THAT by 3 to get the total damage with Ghyrson. So with 10 toughness on the
  board, that's 33 damage to the opponent's face. Most importantly, if you can
  pull this combo off if one of their creatures is indestructible, that means
  they're likely dead without a backup plan, even with infinite life.


  Chandra's Spitfire

  One unsung hero so far of this deck is Chandra's Spitfire which will grow much
  larger than your opponents might think at first if they're unfamiliar. If for
  example you deal 1 damage to each opponent, that's 6 total triggers of its
  ability, giving it +18 power until end of turn. You can use this to
  effectively swing for lethal mid-game if you  play it right.
